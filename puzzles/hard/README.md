# Hardest webpbn puzzles

webpbn puzzles 3867, 13480, 16900, 19080, 25385, 25820, 27174, 30509, 30532,
30654, 30681, 32013 and 32291.  This is the list of the hardest webpbn puzzles
from the [nonogrid](https://github.com/tsionyx/nonogrid) benchmarks.

The puzzles belong to their designers on [webpbn.com](https://webpbn.com) and
may not be redistributed.  `../fetch.sh` downloads them from the webpbn
[export page](https://webpbn.com/export.cgi) in `.nin` format.
